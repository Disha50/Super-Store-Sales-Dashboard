📌 Project Overview

The Super Store Sales Dashboard is an interactive data visualization and business analytics project created to analyze and understand sales and profit performance.
The dashboard provides a clear view of important business metrics such as total sales, total orders, profit, shipping performance, customer segments, payment modes, product categories, sub-categories, monthly sales, and geographical performance.
This project helps transform raw sales data into meaningful and easy-to-understand visual insights for business decision-making.

🎯 Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Understand monthly sales and profit trends.
- Analyze sales based on customer segments.
- Compare different payment modes.
- Analyze different shipping modes.
- Identify the performance of product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze sales and profit across different states.
- Present business information using interactive and easy-to-understand visualizations.
  

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **Data Analysis** – Business performance analysis
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, graphs, maps, and KPI cards
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source and data preparation

📊 Dashboard Features

1. Sales by Segment
The dashboard analyzes sales based on different customer segments:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office
This helps understand which customer segment contributes to overall sales.

2. Sales by Month
The monthly sales visualization shows sales performance throughout the year and helps identify changes and trends in monthly sales.

3. Profit by Month
The profit analysis provides a monthly view of business profitability and helps identify months with higher or lower profit.
 
4. Sales by Payment Mode
Sales are analyzed according to different payment methods, including:
- COD
- Online
- Cards
This provides insights into customer payment preferences.

5. Sales by Ship Mode
The dashboard compares sales across different shipping methods:
- Standard Class
- Second Class
- First Class
- Same Day
This helps analyze the relationship between shipping methods and sales.

6. Sales by Category
Product sales are categorized into:
- Office Supplies
- Technology
- Furniture
This helps identify the contribution of different product categories to total sales.

7. Sales by Sub-Category
The dashboard provides detailed sales analysis for product sub-categories such as:
- Phones
- Chairs
- Binders
- And other products
This provides a more detailed view of product performance.

8. Sales and Profit by State
The geographical map provides a visual representation of sales and profit performance across different states.
This helps identify geographical patterns in business performance.



🔍 Key Insights
The dashboard can be used to identify:
- Overall sales and profit performance.
- Monthly sales and profit trends.
- Customer segment contribution.
- Popular payment methods.
- Shipping mode performance.
- High-performing product categories.
- High-performing product sub-categories.
- Geographical sales and profit distribution.
